📐 Ergonomic Zones Explained

Primary Zone: Place frequently used items (keyboard, mouse, phone)
Secondary Zone: Occasionally used items (notepad, documents)
Monitor Zone: Position screen at arm's length, top at eye level
Warning Zone: Avoid placing items too close to edge
